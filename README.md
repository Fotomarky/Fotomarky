## 🏡 What I'm Building

### [Homestagd.com](https://homestagd.com) — AI Virtual Staging Platform

> Turning empty rooms into compelling spaces — automatically.

A full SaaS product I conceived, designed, built, and launched solo. The full journey: market research, UX design, backend architecture, AI integration, payment systems, multilingual rollout, and growth.

**What it does:**
- Generates photorealistic staged interiors from empty room photos using state-of-the-art image generation models
- Supports style selection, custom prompts, iterative chat-based editing, and 4K HD downloads
- Free watermarked previews (no login) → conversion at the download step
- Stripe-powered credit system with one-time passes and recurring subscriptions

**Tech under the hood:**
`React` · `TypeScript` · `Firebase` · `Cloud Functions` · `fal.ai Seedream v5` · `Stripe` · `i18n (4 languages)`
